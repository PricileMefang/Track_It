Who We Are

TrackIt is a smart expense tracker created out of a real need: growing up as a teen, I often struggled to balance saving for personal projects with unexpected spending on birthdays, family events, and social occasions. That challenge inspired me to build a tool that helps people manage both savings and spending with ease.

Our Mission

We aim to make personal finance simple, accessible, and empowering. TrackIt helps you stay in control of your money so you can save for what matters most without being caught off guard by everyday expenses.

What We Do

TrackIt automatically tracks your expenses, categorizes them, and provides clear insights into your spending habits. It also supports goal based savings, helping you set aside money for projects, events, or personal needs while keeping daily spending in check.

Why Choose TrackIt

Unlike traditional budgeting apps, TrackIt was designed from real-life experience. It doesn’t just record numbers, it understands the balance between saving and living. With smart insights, easy tracking, and a user-friendly design, TrackIt gives you confidence and clarity in your financial journey.
